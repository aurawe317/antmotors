1. Who we are

AutoStock is a mobile application and web service that helps vehicle dealerships manage their inventory, vehicle photos, and deal pipeline. The service is operated by AntMotors ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what personal data we collect through the App, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the data needed to run the service.

2.1 Account & company data

  • Your display name, role, and the branch/office you belong to.
  • Your WhatsApp number and/or phone number, used for team collaboration and sharing listings.
  • Your company name and company logo (if you provide one).
  • Your sign-in credential — stored only as a salted, iteratively hashed value (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256). We never store your password in readable form.

2.2 Inventory & vehicle data

  • Vehicle details you enter: make, model, year, mileage, price, condition, and notes.
  • Vehicle photos and short videos you upload, stored against your company's inventory.

2.3 Device & connection data

  • A session token that keeps you signed in on your device.
  • Basic technical information (device type, app version, approximate request logs) used for security and troubleshooting.

2.4 What we do not collect

  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell your data.
  • We do not access your device's precise GPS location, contacts, or files unless you explicitly choose to upload them.
  • We do not collect payment-card numbers — subscriptions are processed by our payment partner.

3. How we use your information

  • Provide and operate the inventory, photo, and deal-management features.
  • Sync your data securely across your devices and among your team members.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Send service-related notices (e.g., sync status, plan expiry).
  • Improve and protect the service (debugging, abuse prevention).

4. Legal basis (for EEA / UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of a contract (to provide the service you signed up for), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention), and consent (where you explicitly upload content or enable optional features).

5. Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with vetted service providers who help run the product:

  • Hosting & database: Supabase (PostgreSQL) and Cloudflare (hosting, edge). Data is stored in their secured infrastructure.
  • Payments: our payment processor handles billing; we receive only transaction status, not card numbers.

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and property of our users and the public.

6. Where & how we store data

Your data is stored on servers operated by Supabase and Cloudflare. Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. Passwords are protected with PBKDF2 key-stretching, and access is restricted by per-company isolation so one dealership cannot see another's data.

7. Data retention

  • Active accounts: data is kept for as long as your company uses the service.
  • After account deletion, we delete or anonymize your data within 30 days, except where we must retain a limited record for legal or accounting reasons.
  • Session tokens expire automatically.

8. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, inside the App.
  • Correct or update your information in the App settings.
  • Request export or deletion of your company's data by contacting us.
  • Withdraw consent or object to processing where applicable.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@antmotors.app.

9. Children

AutoStock is a business tool for auto dealerships and is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Cookies & local storage

The App uses only minimal local storage on your device to keep you signed in and to cache your inventory for offline use. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the App or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the current version.
